    Load robot, camera, board geometry, and transform parameters.
  2. Detect board state.
    Segment or classify pieces, then convert pixel positions to board coordinates.
  3. Select a shot.
    Generate candidate trajectories and score them by feasibility, collision risk, and expected payoff.
  4. Move robot.

  5. Evaluate outcome.
